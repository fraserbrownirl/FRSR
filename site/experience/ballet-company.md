# Ballet Company

## Structural problem

Audience growth was constrained by conventional ticketing incumbents and marketing channels.

## Insight

Digital acquisition, low-entry pricing, direct ticketing and unconventional partnerships could expand audiences and preserve customer data.

## What Fraser did

Partnered with Groupon, sold thousands of tickets in minutes from €1, bypassed Ticketmaster with in-house ticketing, secured Arts Council and government funding, and partnered with Convention Centre Dublin.

## Status

Annual audience grew from 1,000 to 20,000; performances at 12 theatres; Facebook invited Fraser to its Dublin HQ, according to profile.
