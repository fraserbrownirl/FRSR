# Reel Ukraine

## Structural problem

Ukrainian brands face barriers to representation in foreign markets and are suffering under the issues caused by the war.

## Insight

Many super talented eager Ukrainians found themselves outside of Ukraine and were available to work in such a project.

## What Fraser did

Set up a fashion house in Lisbon, hired two Ukrainians full time, bought large volumes of Ukrainian brand stock and launched the project.

## Status

Currently Reel Ukraine is based in Berlin run by one Ukrainian fashion designer who attends regular pop-ups, street fairs, and other bespoke engagements.
