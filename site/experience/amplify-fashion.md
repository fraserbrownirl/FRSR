# Amplify

## Structural problem

There is a long tail of influence on Instagram where the onboarding tax in terms of communication effort is to high to make it worthwhile engaging accounts with 500 to 7000 followers.

## Insight

The solution creates a new and arguably improved marketing medium for brands or shops.

## What Fraser did

An app where amplifiers and shops create two different types of accounts, AI generates an avatar and allows reaction video creation. Shops select Ampliers pay them and create the video in the app. Ampliers then post the video tagging the shop.

## Status

Live. 40 accounts signed up and three shops.
